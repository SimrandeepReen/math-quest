Mia's Playground World (V4)

A small persistent play world for iPad where arithmetic is embedded in activities instead of presented as a worksheet.

Rooms:
- Home: backpack and toy activities
- Candy Shop: choosing and sharing treats
- Playground: scoring and picnic activities

Gameplay:
- Touch/pointer drag-and-drop interactions
- Number entry instead of multiple-choice guessing
- Coins and persistent treasure collection
- Free-play mode with draggable toys/treasures
- Adaptive maths stages hidden from the child
- Gentle celebration sounds

Current maths stages:
1. Two-digit +/- single-digit, result stays two-digit
2. Crossing ten in either direction
3. Two-step mixed addition/subtraction, bounded 0-20

Hosted with GitHub Pages.
